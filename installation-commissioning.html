Service Overview

Flawless Execution from Rooftop to the Grid

48-Hour Execution

Physical mounting completed in just 2 days with zero disruption to your daily life.

Certified Electricians

MNRE-trained technicians with 16+ years of specialized solar experience.

Dual Earth Pits

Commissioning Certificate

Full DISCOM inspection report and official net-meter synchronization.

At Solarfi, our installation teams are certified by the National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE) and MNRE. We treat your rooftop with utmost care: using non-destructive clamps, industrial torque wrenches on all bolts, and weatherproof conduit piping. Once mounted, we synchronize your bi-directional meter with the APSPDCL grid.

  • Rigid Structural Anchoring Tailored to RCC & Sheet Roofs
  • Dedicated AC/DC Segregated Conduits with Fire-Retardant Sleeving
  • Chemical Earthing Pit Installation (< 2 Ohms Resistance)
  • DISCOM Net-Meter Synchronization & Official Handover
Execution Workflow

How We Deliver Installation & Commissioning

Our standardized engineering protocol guaranteeing safety, durability, and maximum generation in Andhra Pradesh.

01
Structure Assembly
Setting galvanized pillars, purlins, and rafter beams to precision tilt.
02
Panel Clamping
Mounting Tier-1 panels with stainless mid-clamps and end-clamps.
03
Wiring & Earthing
Connecting DC strings, junction boxes, and separate chemical earth pits.
04
Grid Sync
Joint inspection with DISCOM assistant engineers and bi-directional test.
