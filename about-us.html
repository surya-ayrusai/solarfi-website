Company Overview

Smart Energy Begins with Trusted Execution

Solarfi is an innovation-driven solar EPC company based in Pulivendula, Andhra Pradesh. We specialize in delivering turnkey solar solutions that are efficient, compliant, and durable—whether you are powering a single-family home under PM Surya Ghar, a commercial business, or an agricultural irrigation facility.

With 16+ years of specialized solar experience, our certified engineering crew handles every step in-house: site analysis with drone and LiDAR simulations, DISCOM net-metering sanctions with APSPDCL, certified installation within 48 hours, and 24/7 generation tracking.
