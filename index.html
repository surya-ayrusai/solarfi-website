Your Questions, Answered

Any Indian homeowner with a valid electricity connection and rooftop space can apply under the PM Surya Ghar Yojana. Both urban and rural homeowners across all states are eligible.

For a 3kW system, you can receive up to ₹78,000 under PM Surya Ghar, ₹30,000/kW for the first 2kW and ₹18,000/kW for the 3rd kW.

You'll need your Aadhaar card, electricity bill (last 3 months), bank passbook, and a recent passport-size photo. Solarfi handles all the paperwork for you.

Once approvals are in place, our certified team completes installation in just 48 hours. The total process from inquiry to live system is typically 7–14 days.
