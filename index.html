Your Questions, Answered

Any Indian homeowner with a valid electricity connection and rooftop space can apply under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana. Both urban and rural homeowners across all states (including all APSPDCL districts) are fully eligible.

For a 3kW residential system, you receive a flat ₹78,000 central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar — ₹30,000/kW for the first 2kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW. Solarfi credits this directly into your bank account through the national DBT portal.

You need your Aadhaar card, electricity bill (last 3 months), bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and a passport-size photo. Solarfi takes care of 100% of the portal submissions and paperwork for you.

Once structural and load clearances are in place, our certified team completes the physical rooftop installation in just 48 hours. Total time from first survey to grid-tied live power is typically 7–14 days.

Standard on-grid systems shut down during grid outages for lineman safety. If you need backup during power cuts, Solarfi supplies and configures hybrid inverters paired with lithium battery storage.
